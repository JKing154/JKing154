The Brief

The wheel had to fit inside front roll hoop clearance, work for every hand size, and last a full competition day. ARG26 didn't need buttons on the wheel, so I could focus on strength and comfort.

Design

carbon fiber composite sandwich

quick-disconnect onto the column spline, greased to get rid of last year's slop

FINDING
Each added ply per side ≈ +100 lbf yield for only 10–20 g
The thicker 0.75" core was strongest at 2000 N, but not worth the extra 13 g and thickness.
LESSON
An unremoved adhesive backing sheet delaminated several panels
I didn't catch it until they came out of the oven. Since you can't see inside the bag, every layup step has to be done carefully.

Driver Feedback

The grip shape came from a comfort survey drivers filled out after each session. They rated each contact point, and I used those ratings to update the CAD.

HertzTrap: Spotted Lanternfly Zapper

MAE 2250 Mechanical Design · Client: E&J Gallo Winery
People's Choice Award
VOTED BY PEERS + FACULTY, FULL MAE 2250 CLASS
<15 s
ONE-PERSON RELOCATE
60 Hz
LURE FREQUENCY
6 lb
TOTAL WEIGHT

A trap that attracts lanternflies with a 60 Hz signal and kills them on an electrified mesh. It doesn't use pesticides, and nobody has to remove the bugs by hand.

FINISHED DEVICE
DESIGN POSTER

The Problem

Spotted lanternflies are devastating Northeast vineyards with no natural predators. The client needed them intercepted before they reach the vines.

How It Works

Lure
57 mm speaker, Arduino Nano + amp, continuous 60 Hz (from a USDA attraction study)
Kill mesh
dense inner copper mesh and a coarser outer mesh at opposite polarity. A bug touching both completes the circuit
Enclosure
~8×8×2 in, 3D printed, weatherproof
Deployment
4 adjustable legs, staked and leveled at canopy height, 6 lb total

Test Results

  • Structural: held 40 lb on the top (target 30) and survived 5–10 lb drops from a foot
  • Adjustability: stable up to 3 ft, near vine canopy height
  • Portability: one person can move and reset it in under 15 s, so 100+ units a day is doable
  • Electrical: arc discharge between mesh layers confirmed on the bench
  • Open question: we never tested it on live insects, so the real kill rate is still unknown

Beach E-Bike Frame: FEA Verification & Validation

Structural Mechanics Simulation (FEA) · Class Project · Cornell

~2×

No-go
BEACH-BIKE VERDICT

Could a beach cruiser e-bike work for a bike-share program in beachside cities? My first model looked fine, but checking it gave a different answer.

VON MISES, CONVERGED MESH

The Question

A class assignment to learn FEA verification and validation: design a new bike frame for a renewable bike-share program. I wanted to see if a beach cruiser style frame could work, maybe for bike-share in beachside cities.

The Setup

Concept
beach-cruiser style e-bike frame for bike-share
Model
thin-walled tubes modeled with shell elements, which are more accurate in bending and much cheaper to run than solids
Loads
rider forces at seat and pedals, plus a 3 kg battery
Material
aluminum alloy

What the Analysis Said

FIRST ANSWER · 4 MM MESH
29 MPa peak, which looked fine
The coarse mesh smeared the stress at the sharp tube junctions and made the frame look safer than it was.
VERIFIED · CONVERGED MESH
~56 MPa, almost double
The two results didn't agree. The converged one showed the beach bike plan wasn't a good idea.

What I Learned

LESSON 01
Always check that the mesh has converged
The first run gave me a number that looked right but was about half the real value. The convergence study is what caught it.
LESSON 02
FEA can rule out an idea early
The beach bike idea didn't hold up, and it's a lot better to find that out in FEA than after building bikes.
